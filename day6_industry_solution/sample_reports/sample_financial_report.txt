ACME TECH CORP - FISCAL YEAR 2025 FINANCIAL AUDIT REPORT

Executive Overview:
Acme Tech Corp delivered strong operational performance in FY2025, driven by accelerating adoption of enterprise cloud infrastructure and AI developer tooling. Total consolidated revenue reached $150,000,000, representing a 24% year-over-year increase compared to FY2024 ($121,000,000).

Financial Performance Metrics:
- Total Revenue: $150,000,000
- Operating Expenses: $95,000,000
- Net Income: $35,000,000
- Total Assets: $210,000,000
- Total Liabilities: $70,000,000
- Cash & Equivalents: $45,000,000

Segment Performance:
1. Enterprise Cloud Solutions: Revenue of $90,000,000 (+30% YoY). High margin cloud subscriptions.
2. AI Developer Tools: Revenue of $40,000,000 (+45% YoY). Fast growing segment.
3. Legacy Software Licensing: Revenue of $20,000,000 (-10% YoY). Managed decline.

Key Risk Factors:
1. Increased GPU hardware supply chain latency and semiconductor cost inflation.
2. Escalating cybersecurity threats and data privacy compliance mandates across EU regions.
3. High customer acquisition cost (CAC) in competitive mid-market segments.

Strategic Guidance & FY2026 Outlook:
Management projects full-year FY2026 revenue between $180M and $190M. Capital allocation will prioritize R&D for next-generation agentic AI products while expanding enterprise direct sales capacity.
